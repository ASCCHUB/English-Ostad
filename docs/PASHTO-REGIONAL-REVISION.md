# Swat / Dir / Malakand Pashto revision

The user selected Swat / Dir / Malakand everyday Pashto. This revision targets that region and uses short instructions addressed to one child. Shared Pashto words remain where suitable. Pakistani Pashto is regionally diverse; this is a revised draft, not native-speaker certification.

## Applied changes

| Try again. | بیا هڅه وکړئ | بیا کوشش وکړه |

School wording is updated in vocabulary, everyday phrases, reading, grammar and stories. Roman Pashto is revised alongside script text; broken forms such as `Sh wo on dzay`, `khowandzi`, `keg dai` and `Awr edalay` have been removed from active copy. The convention is a practical northern conversational reading aid (for example `kha`, `zama`, `bya`, `school`, `kitaab`), not IPA or standardized romanization.

Vocabulary, instructions, all 21 grammar examples, the 15 story lines and 16 phonics notes have been reviewed as part of this revision. Reviewing these with an AI does not replace a fluent local speaker's review. English, Urdu and Roman Urdu learning strings were checked against the pre-revision hash and remain unchanged. Word IDs, answer keys and saved-progress keys remain stable.

Pashto text is tagged `ps-PK`. English phonics words inside RTL notes use explicit LTR isolation. The language selector identifies the Swat/Dir target. Regional policy and source scopes are recorded in `src/ustad/pashtoLocale.js`; the language-review export includes this metadata.

## Reference checks

- [Mashaal Radio: a school in Swat](https://www.mashaalradio.com/a/33123271.html) attests local reporting usage of **سکول**. This supports the word choice, not approval of the entire translation.
- [University of Peshawar Pashto Academy: lexical variation study](https://pashtoacademy.edu.pk/journal/pdf/2015-Pasht-44-Kaleem-LexicalVar.pdf) has a search-indexed excerpt describing **Kashmiry** for tomato among Swat speakers. The full PDF fetch was unavailable. The app retains **ټماټر** alongside it, because household preferences vary.
- [Open Pashto-English Dictionary: پۍ](https://oped.univie.ac.at/oped.php?alph=2&dev=1&dir=0&entry=12616&how=1&let=&nons=1&what=1) labels this milk term Eastern.
- [thePashto dictionary: potato](https://thepashto.com/word.php?english=potato) includes **الو** among its variants.

Other rewritten sentences are editorial drafts and have not been independently certified. These references cannot establish a single vocabulary for every family in Swat, Dir and Malakand. A local speaker should confirm remaining vocabulary, grammatical naturalness and Roman Pashto before release.
