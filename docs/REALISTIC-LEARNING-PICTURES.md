# Realistic learning pictures

The active app now uses local realistic pictures for all 185 vocabulary entries and all existing First Steps picture cues (236 labels including aliases, colors and quantities).

- 197 optimized new images in public/learning-photos, totaling about 9.5 MB, plus existing apple, ball, book and companion artwork.
- Shared WordPicture renderer used by First Steps, picture-word games, vocabulary, spelling, writing, letter lessons, quizzes and story word banks.
- Exact apple counts for one to ten, recolored balls for colors, and two examples for plural hands/feet.
- Pictures are decorative within existing accessible card labels; picture-only quiz choices retain neutral labels. Loading errors show a neutral placeholder, never the written answer. Changing words resets image error state.
- Photo credits are available from the app footer at /learning-photos/credits.html; machine-readable attribution is in credits.json. Original photographs retain their linked source licenses. Resizing is documented there.
- The production offline manifest includes all 197 photos and both credit files.

63 assets were created with built-in OpenAI image generation in realistic photographic style. Generated people are fictional. Exact prompts and preserved original paths are recorded in [generation-prompts.json](realistic-pictures/generation-prompts.json). Published images are resized copies; original generated files remain in place.

Validation: 129 tests pass; 236 image renderings and exact quantity counts pass; all 1,065 First Steps task views render; production build succeeds. A local headless Chrome preview checks representative pictures in light and dark themes. Touch, voice and WebGL behavior were outside this image-only change.
