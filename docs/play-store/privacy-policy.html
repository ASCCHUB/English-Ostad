English Ustad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

English Ustad is an app that helps children and beginners learn English with help in Pashto and Urdu. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app does not collect, send or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. English Ustad has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives information from the app.

Information stored on your device

To remember progress, the app saves the following only on your own phone:

  • the learner's nickname (any name you choose; a real name is not needed),
  • lessons completed, stars, scores and settings (language, sounds, starting level).

This information never leaves the device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission only when a grown-up or learner taps a recording button (for example "Say it" practice, or a parent recording the letter sounds). Recordings stay on the device so they can be played back. They are never uploaded, shared or sent to us or to anyone else. Speaking practice is off until a parent turns it on in the Parent area.

Speech

Pashto, letter sounds and (when included) the Pakistani English and Urdu voices are recordings that come inside the app. Other English words are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech voice, using your device's speech settings. The app does not send the learner's words or recordings anywhere to be spoken.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the app opens WhatsApp, email or your phone's share menu with a message containing a link to the app. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and it is sent by that app, not by English Ustad.

Children

English Ustad is designed for children and families. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links to social media inside the learning lessons. Settings that change the course are in a Parent area.

Third parties

The app does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
